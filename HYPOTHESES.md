# HYPOTHESES

Use stable IDs. Never silently delete rejected hypotheses; mark them rejected and record why.

## Status vocabulary

- `OPEN` — plausible but insufficiently tested
- `SUPPORTED` — supported by current evidence, not necessarily proven
- `WEAKENED` — evidence reduced confidence
- `REJECTED` — falsified or no longer viable under current evidence
- `SUPERSEDED` — replaced by a better hypothesis
